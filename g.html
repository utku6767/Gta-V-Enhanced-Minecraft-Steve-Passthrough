Short description
This mod bridges what you do in your Minecraft instance to GTA V, and the ReShade mod lets you see your actions in-game.

Features and bugs
Features:
  • Build in Los Santos: Blocks you place become invisible GTA props, so people and cars stop at your walls.
  • TNT and creepers: Blow up in both games. Every Minecraft explosion is also a GTA explosion.
  • Minecraft weapons, GTA effects: Arrows land as GTA bullets, crossbow fireworks burst as GTA rockets, sword swings send people flying and shove cars, and ender pearls move GTA's player.
  • Elytra flight: Minecraft physics fly Steve, and GTA follows with a chase camera. Double-tap Space auto-launches you 10 blocks high for immediate takeoff.
  • Mobs vs police: Hostile mobs hunt GTA's people, the police shoot back, and damage crosses over both ways.
  • Iron Golems & Wolves (Defenders): Tamed wolves and Iron Golems hunt hostile police and armed assailants. They spawn GTA double peds so police and firearms can shoot them, backed by proactive 3x3 barrier foundations and anti-fall ground anchors.
  • Native Survival & Shields: Steve is spawned in Creative but can toggle Survival mode. GTA V bullet damage bridges to Minecraft. Blocking with a shield neutralizes police bullets, otherwise Steve takes raw damage.
  • The Nether & Returning Home: Walk through a lit portal and the ground around it turns into the Nether, GTA's sky goes red, and the clock races to midnight. Stepping back into the portal for 1 second safely restores the original terrain top-down, despawns Nether mobs, and resets the clock to noon.
  • Nether Bed Explosions: Interacting with or sleeping in beds during Nether mode causes massive explosions that bridge to GTA V.
  • Boss Encounters (Wither & Ender Dragon): Full aerial combat bosses over Los Santos with anti-fall protection: Wither executes strafing runs at a locked 3.5-block combat altitude; Ender Dragon executes 3-phase tactical CAS (orbiting fireball barrages, street-sweeping dives that ragdoll peds with 30.0f force, and rapid climb recovery).
  • Water Current Ragdolls: Minecraft flowing water and source blocks transfer velocity vectors to GTA peds, knocking them into ragdoll and flushing them downstream until they exit the water.
  • Pause / Break Mode & Virtual Mouse: Press Pause/Break to block GTA menus and car entry. Use F to swap offhand items, E to open inventory with a Virtual Mouse (Arrow keys move, Enter clicks), and T to open Minecraft chat with typing redirection, message history, and Tab auto-completion.
  • Enhanced GTA Weapon Handling: Press Tab to cycle GTA weapons (Carbine Rifle, Minigun, RPG) directly in Steve's hands with custom hand fitting, eliminated double-gun visuals, and accurate muzzle-to-crosshair ballistics.
  • Aim Snapping: Snaps the player teleport accurately to the aimed crosshair location (Toggle via Page Down key).
  • Elytra Auto-launch: Enabling elytra flight via double tapping Space instantly teleports you 10 blocks high for immediate takeoff.
  • Skin Swapping: Press NumLock to hot-swap your skin between Steve and a custom skin. Place custom_skin.png and default_skin.png in a passthrough folder alongside your mods folder (i.e., ../passthrough/).

Installation instructions
Requirements: You will need to install ScriptHookV, OpenRPF.asi, ReShade's dxgi.dll (Available via the official ReShade Setup), and the Fabric API mod for 26.3.

  1. Put the files from the "GTA V" folder into your GTA V Enhanced folder.
  2. Put the files from the "Minecraft" folder into your Minecraft mods folder.
  3. Launch both GTA V Enhanced and Minecraft Fabric 26.3 at the same time.
  4. Press F7 in GTA V Enhanced to activate the mod.

Abandoned Experiments: Fishing Hook

The fishing hook feature was attempted but eventually abandoned. In theory, it was designed to work as follows:

  • The Minecraft FishingHook entity would successfully hit the invisible proxy entity representing a GTA ped.
  • Upon connecting, the Fabric mod would send a "hook" JSON message to GTA V via WebSocket.
  • When the player triggered the rod's pull/retrieve action, a "reel" message would be sent.
  • The C++ ASI script would respond by ragdolling the ped and applying a physical impulse (ApplyForceToEntityWithOffset).
  • Depending on the player's relative angle, directional force would be applied to the ped's legs: pulling from the front would apply force from the back of the legs to ragdoll them forward. Pulling from vertically above would apply an upward force directly through the feet, launching the ragdolled ped straight up towards the player.
